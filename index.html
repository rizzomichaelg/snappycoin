Frequently Asked Questions

What payment methods do the machines accept?

The machines accept coins; there are money changers available on-site. We also accept DexterPay for secure, mobile payments. If you want to set it up before you arrive, our location number is 12105.

Do you offer detergent if I forget mine?

Absolutely! Our vending machines have detergent, fabric softener, and dryer sheets.

Can I leave my clothes unattended?

We recommend staying on-site for the safety and care of your items. We are not responsible for items left unattended.

Do you have drop-off laundry service?

Yes! You may call in advance or just drop off your laundry for a quick turnaround.
