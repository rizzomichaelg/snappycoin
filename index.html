Straight answers

Before you load the car.

Still unsure? Call (314) 628-1001 and an attendant can help.

What payments do the machines accept?+

Use coins, the on-site changers, or DexterPay. Snappy’s DexterPay location number is 12105.

Can I wash a king-size comforter?+

Yes. Our largest washers hold up to 80 pounds and are built for comforters, bedding, and other bulky loads.

Do you sell detergent and laundry supplies?+

Yes. Vending machines carry detergent, fabric softener, dryer sheets, drinks, and snacks.

How does pickup and delivery work?+

Start by checking your address. If a route is available, choose a pickup window and preferences, verify your phone, save a card securely, and submit the request. You are charged after the laundry is weighed.

Can I leave while my clothes wash?+

We recommend staying with your items. Snappy is not responsible for laundry left unattended.
