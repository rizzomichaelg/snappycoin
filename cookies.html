Cookie Statement

Effective date: June 19, 2026

This Cookie Statement explains how Snappy Coin Laundry uses cookies, pixels, local storage, session storage, and similar technologies on our website.

Cookie Choices

When you first visit the site, a cookie banner lets you accept or decline optional analytics and advertising cookies. Meta Pixel and Google Analytics are loaded only after optional cookies are accepted. Essential security, anti-spam, form, and preference tools may still run because they are needed to operate the site and promotion.

What These Technologies Do

Cookies and similar technologies help websites remember information, protect forms, measure activity, and support advertising. Local storage and session storage are browser storage features that can keep limited information on your device.

Technologies We Use

  • Essential and security tools: Cloudflare and Turnstile help protect forms, reduce spam, and keep the website available.
  • Promo attribution storage: The promo form stores first-touch attribution, such as landing URL, referrer, UTM parameters, and click IDs, in browser storage so the source of a claim can be submitted with the form.
  • Meta Pixel: Meta Pixel measures page views and, after a successful coupon claim, a standard Lead event with generic campaign metadata.
  • Google Analytics: Google Analytics helps us understand website traffic and page activity.
  • Embedded or linked services: Google Maps, DexterLive status services, and social media links may use their own technologies when loaded or opened.

Meta Lead Event

The promo claim flow sends Meta a Lead event only after the backend confirms that the coupon claim was completed successfully. The event uses the content name "Snappy Promo Coupon Claim." It does not include your name, email address, phone number, verification code, or coupon code.

Your Choices

You can accept or decline optional cookies in the site banner. Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies and site data. Browser privacy settings, ad blockers, and platform-level privacy controls may limit pixels or analytics tools. If you block scripts, cookies, local storage, or security tools, some forms or verification steps may not work correctly.
