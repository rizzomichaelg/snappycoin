Cookie Statement

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This Cookie Statement explains how Snappy Coin Laundry uses cookies, pixels, local storage, session storage, and similar technologies on our website.

Analytics And Advertising

Our booking site (book.snappycoinlaundry.com) and our main website (snappycoinlaundry.com) use Google Analytics and Google Ads conversion measurement. Our main website also uses the Meta Pixel, to measure ads on Facebook and Instagram. These tools set cookies so we can learn which pages and ads lead to bookings. Neither site has a cookie banner: the tools load for every visitor.

We don't send Google Analytics, Google Ads, or Meta your name, phone number, email address, or street address. We don't use Google remarketing — ads shown to you because you visited our sites. We may use information collected by the Meta Pixel to show ads on Facebook and Instagram, including to people who have visited our main website.

You can opt out:

  • Google Analytics: install Google's opt-out browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
  • Google ads: turn off personalized ads for your Google account at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Meta: in Facebook or Instagram, go to Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Your activity off Meta technologies, and disconnect activity from other websites.
  • Your browser: you can block or delete cookies in your browser's settings. The booking site needs its own cookie to verify your phone, so blocking all cookies will stop booking from working.

What These Technologies Do

Cookies and similar technologies help websites remember information, protect forms, measure activity, and support advertising. Local storage and session storage are browser storage features that can keep limited information on your device.

Technologies We Use

  • Essential and security tools: Cloudflare and Turnstile help protect forms, reduce spam, and keep the website available.
  • Promo attribution storage: The promo form stores first-touch attribution, such as landing URL, referrer, UTM parameters, Facebook click IDs, Meta ad parameters, Google Ads click IDs and ValueTrack parameters, and other ad click IDs, in browser storage so the source of a claim can be submitted with the form. If Meta or Google advertising cookies exist, the form may also submit browser ID and click ID cookie values with the attribution payload.
  • Meta Pixel: Meta Pixel measures page views and, after a successful coupon claim, a standard Lead event with generic campaign metadata.
  • Google measurement: Google Analytics and Google Ads help us understand website traffic, page activity, and advertising performance.
  • Embedded or linked services: Google Maps, DexterLive status services, and social media links may use their own technologies when loaded or opened.

Meta Lead Event

The promo claim flow sends Meta a Lead event only after the backend confirms that the coupon claim was completed successfully. The event uses the content name "Snappy Promo Coupon Claim." It does not include your name, email address, phone number, verification code, or coupon code.

Your Choices

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies and site data. Browser privacy settings, ad blockers, and platform-level privacy controls may limit pixels or analytics tools. You can also use the opt-outs listed under Analytics And Advertising. If you block scripts, cookies, local storage, or security tools, some forms or verification steps may not work correctly.
