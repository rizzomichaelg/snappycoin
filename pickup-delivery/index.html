Common questions

Before your first pickup

What does the service-area waitlist do?

It records your address and contact details for staff review. It does not create an order, reserve a pickup, or promise that service will expand. Staff can invite you to finish a verified booking when an approved window is available.

How is the final price calculated?

Laundry is weighed after pickup and charged at the promotional price of $1.35 per pound, with a $15 minimum, through August 31st. The regular price is $1.50 per pound.

When will my laundry be returned?

Choose your pickup first, then select from the available delivery days and times.

Can I request fragrance-free detergent?

Yes. Choose “Free & clear” under Laundry preferences and add any sensitivities in Special instructions.

What if I miss the pickup?

Call (314) 628-1001 as soon as possible so the local team can review the available options.

What bags and items can I send?

Call the store before booking if you need bag guidance or want to send an unusual item.

Other services (comforters, blankets, etc.): Call for pricing.

What if an item is lost or damaged?

Call the store and use the private order page to contact support so the team can review the order details.
