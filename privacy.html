Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Snappy Coin Laundry collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website, join our email list, or claim a promotional coupon. Our pickup-and-delivery service and its booking site, book.snappycoinlaundry.com, are covered by their own Privacy Policy; both policies describe the same analytics and advertising tools.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly, including:

  • Name, email address, mobile phone number, and ZIP code for promo coupon claims.
  • Email address and first name for newsletter or marketing list signups.
  • Marketing consent choices, SMS verification consent, coupon claim status, and related timestamps.
  • Attribution information such as UTM parameters, referrer, landing page, Facebook click IDs, Meta ad parameters, Google Ads click IDs and ValueTrack parameters, and other click identifiers.

We may also collect technical information from your browser, such as device and browser details, page views, approximate usage activity, and security challenge results.

How We Use Information

  • To verify your phone number before issuing a promotional coupon.
  • To email coupon codes and service-related promo messages.
  • To prevent duplicate coupon claims and protect the promotion from abuse.
  • To manage email marketing subscriptions and unsubscribe requests.
  • To measure advertising performance and improve our website.
  • To comply with legal, security, audit, and operational requirements.

SMS Verification And Marketing Consent

On this website, Snappy Coin Laundry uses text messages only for verification: a one-time code to verify that a mobile number belongs to you. Optional text updates about pickup-and-delivery orders are covered by the Privacy Policy and SMS Terms & Conditions on our booking site. Consent to receive a one-time verification text is separate from marketing consent, and we do not treat SMS verification consent as consent to receive SMS marketing. We do not send marketing or promotional text messages.

Email marketing consent is optional and must be selected separately. Marketing emails include an unsubscribe option.

Message frequency varies with your verification requests. Each request normally sends one verification text; requesting another code sends an additional text. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier and plan.

Reply STOP to any Snappy text message to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help.

Snappy Coin Laundry does not sell, rent, or share mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We provide mobile information to service providers only as necessary to operate the services you requested — for example, to send messages, host the website, or process orders. Those service providers may use the information only to provide those services to Snappy or as otherwise required by law.

How We Protect Promo Data

The promo system is designed so lookup and duplicate-prevention checks use hashed identifiers. Sensitive contact details and coupon codes should not be stored in plain text by the backend. Staff redemption screens should show only the information needed to validate a coupon, such as phone last four digits, and staff actions should be logged.

Service Providers

We may use service providers to operate the website and promotion, including:

  • Cloudflare for website hosting, Workers, D1 database, bot prevention, and security services.
  • Twilio Verify for one-time phone verification codes.
  • Resend for transactional coupon email. Marketing consent and unsubscribe suppression remain recorded in Cloudflare D1.
  • Meta Pixel, Google Analytics, and Google Ads for advertising and website measurement.
  • Google Maps for displaying our store location.
  • DexterLive status services for machine availability information.

Advertising And Analytics

Our booking site (book.snappycoinlaundry.com) and our main website (snappycoinlaundry.com) use Google Analytics and Google Ads conversion measurement. Our main website also uses the Meta Pixel, to measure ads on Facebook and Instagram. These tools set cookies so we can learn which pages and ads lead to bookings. Neither site has a cookie banner: the tools load for every visitor.

We don't send Google Analytics, Google Ads, or Meta your name, phone number, email address, or street address. We don't use Google remarketing — ads shown to you because you visited our sites. We may use information collected by the Meta Pixel to show ads on Facebook and Instagram, including to people who have visited our main website.

You can opt out:

  • Google Analytics: install Google's opt-out browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.
  • Google ads: turn off personalized ads for your Google account at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Meta: in Facebook or Instagram, go to Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Your activity off Meta technologies, and disconnect activity from other websites.
  • Your browser: you can block or delete cookies in your browser's settings. The booking site needs its own cookie to verify your phone, so blocking all cookies will stop booking from working.

For the promo claim flow, we send Meta a standard Lead event only after a coupon claim is completed successfully. We do not send your name, email, phone number, verification code, or coupon code to Meta in that event.

The promo form may send advertising attribution metadata to our backend with your claim, including Facebook click IDs, Meta ad parameters, Google Ads click IDs and ValueTrack parameters, and Meta or Google advertising cookie identifiers when those identifiers exist.

Unsubscribing

You can unsubscribe from marketing emails using the unsubscribe link in any marketing email. We may keep a suppression record so we can honor your unsubscribe choice.

Data Retention

We keep information only as long as reasonably needed for the promotion, audit records, fraud prevention, legal compliance, and ordinary business operations.

Your Choices

You can choose not to submit a promo claim or marketing signup form. You can opt out of Google Analytics, Google ad personalization, and Meta's use of your activity on our website as described under Advertising And Analytics, and you can adjust browser settings to limit cookies or tracking technologies. Some security, verification, or form features may not work if cookies, scripts, or third-party services are blocked.
